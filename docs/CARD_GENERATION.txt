┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1.  THE SCHEMA  —  one card, exactly this shape, nothing more                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

{id:'', n:'', e:'', f:'', r:'', t:'', lvl:0, atk:0, def:0, txt:'', eff:'', fx:''}

FIELD   TYPE     RULES
─────── ──────── ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
id      string   Unique across the WHOLE game (cards.js + set2.js + anything new).
                 lowercase a–z and digits only, no spaces, no hyphens.
                 4–12 chars. Maltese word preferred — it's the internal handle.
                 e.g. 'hanut', 'sikkina', 'bajtra', 'kannol2'
                 ⚠ Duplicate ids silently break deck building. Always grep first.

n       string   Display name, English, Title Case.
                 ≤ 22 chars keeps it on ONE line. Hard cap 34.
                 ALL CAPS is reserved for LEGENDARY cards only ('THE KUNJATA').
                 Apostrophes must be escaped in JS: 'Nanna\'s Slipper'

e       string   ONE emoji. The card art. See §6 for the selection rule.

f       string   Attribute. EXACTLY one of:
                 'festa' | 'razzett' | 'belt' | 'bahar' | 'hazen'
                 or  null  for spells and traps. (null, not '' and not 'none'.)
                   festa   🎆 FESTA   — loud, fast, explosive
                   razzett 🐇 FARM    — steady, brutal, no nonsense
                   belt    🏰 CITY    — slow, annoying, unkillable
                   bahar   🌊 SEA     — sunburn and disappointment
                   hazen   😈 TROUBLE — the properly rude ones

r       string   Rarity. EXACTLY one of:
                 'komuni' (Common) | 'rari' (Rare) | 'epiku' (Epic) | 'leggendarju' (Legendary)

t       string   'monster' | 'spell' | 'trap'

lvl     integer  Monsters: 1–8.   Spells and traps: ALWAYS 0.
                 1–4 = free summon · 5–6 = 1 tribute · 7–8 = 2 tributes

atk     integer  Monsters: 0–3000, multiple of 100 (50-steps tolerated).
                 Spells and traps: ALWAYS 0.

def     integer  Monsters: 0–3000, multiple of 100.
                 Spells and traps: ALWAYS 0.

txt     string   THE JOKE. Pure flavour. Contains NO game mechanics.
                 ≤ 105 chars recommended. HARD CAP 116 — past that it clips.

eff     string   WHAT THE CARD DOES, in plain player English. One sentence.
                 ≤ 85 chars recommended. HARD CAP 91.
                 If the card has no effect, write a one-line role description:
                   'No effect — pure wall.'   'No effect — efficient level-3 attacker.'
                   'No effect — straight level-4 beater.'
                 Must MATCH the fx exactly. Never promise what fx cannot do.

fx      string   Engine hook. '' (empty) = vanilla monster.
                 ⚠ ONLY reuse an existing fx. A new fx string does nothing until
                 someone writes engine code for it.

                 MONSTER fx (22): boom burn cleave discard double drain grow
                     grow2 heal heal8 healall kamikaze leech peek pierce revive
                     shield snipe stun taunt thorns weaken
                 SPELL fx (9):  s_buff s_buffall s_destroy s_discard s_draw
                     s_draw1 s_heal s_search s_steal
                 TRAP fx (6):   t_bounce t_burn t_destroy t_mirror t_negate t_weaken

INVARIANTS (a card that breaks one of these is broken)
  • t !== 'monster'  ⟹  lvl === 0 && atk === 0 && def === 0 && f === null
  • t === 'monster'  ⟹  1 <= lvl <= 8 && f is one of the five attributes
  • fx === ''        ⟹  the card renders as a NORMAL (sand) frame
  • fx !== ''        ⟹  the card renders as an EFFECT (orange) frame
  • eff and txt are two DIFFERENT strings. Never repeat one inside the other.

  3  │    0     │  600–1600   │  600–1900   │  1900–2800    │ 2500
  4  │    0     │  700–2000   │  200–2100   │  2200–3400    │ 2900
  5  │    1     │ 2000–2300   │ 1500–2500   │  3800–4500    │ 4200
  6  │    1     │ 2200–2600   │ 1200–2800   │  3700–5200    │ 4500
  7  │    2     │ 2700–2900   │ 2400–2900   │  5200–5600    │ 5400
  8  │    2     │ 2800–3000   │ 2200–2900   │  5000–5800    │ 5500
       (measured across the live pool: cards.js + set2.js + set3.js = 200 cards)

  • A card with a STRONG fx sits at the BOTTOM of its total range.
  • A vanilla card (fx: '') may sit at the TOP.
  • Glass cannons are fine and funny: high ATK / tiny DEF ('Firework Finale' 2000/200).
  • Walls are fine and funny: tiny ATK / high DEF ('Sunburnt Tourist' 600/1800).
  • 3000 ATK is the ceiling. Nothing beats THE KUNJATA on raw numbers.
  • Never exceed the level's max total. Level 4 with 2000/2100 is a bug, not a card.

RARITY DISTRIBUTION TARGETS (per new batch, and across the whole game)

  RARITY       │ live % │ pack odds │ dust │ what it means
 ──────────────┼────────┼───────────┼──────┼───────────────────────────────────
  komuni       │   55%  │   60%     │   5  │ vanilla or one small effect, lvl 1–4
  rari         │   28%  │   27%     │  20  │ a real effect, lvl 3–5
  epiku        │   13%  │   10%     │  60  │ a swing effect, lvl 5–6
  leggendarju  │    4%  │    3%     │ 200  │ lvl 7–8, game-defining, ONE per deck

  Legendary quota: at most ONE new legendary per attribute per set.
  A batch of 10 cards should be roughly: 5 common · 3 rare · 2 epic · 0–1 legendary.

CARD TYPE MIX  (live pool: 140 monster / 39 spell / 21 trap)
  Aim for  70% monster · 19% spell · 11% trap  in any new batch.

DECK-BUILDING LIMITS (these are engine rules — do not contradict them)
  • Deck size 40 · max 3 copies of any card · starting LP 8000
  • Legendaries are almost always run as 1-of. Design them so 1-of feels right.
  • The counter triangle gives +500 ATK when you attack the attribute you counter:
      FESTA → CITY → FARM → FESTA   and   FARM → SEA → TROUBLE → FESTA
    Do not design a card that hard-counters an attribute; that's the triangle's job.

WHO IS TALKING
  A Maltese adult in their 30s–50s, deadpan, at a table outside a band club,
  telling you about their week. They are not trying to be funny. That's why it lands.

THE FORMULA THAT WORKS
  SETUP (a true, specific, mundane Maltese detail)
  + TURN (one extra beat that reveals the whole culture)
  = the joke.

  'Took nine months to build. Lasts one afternoon. Bit like some marriages.'
  'Factor 50 was right there in the shop. He walked past it.'
  '247 unread. Six of them are the same photo. Two are prayers.'
  'Started in March. It is now November. "Next week, sur."'

  Notice: no punchline word, no "LOL", no exclamation marks. Specificity IS the joke.

RULES OF THE VOICE
  ✔ English sentences with Maltese words sprinkled — ġbejna, luzzu, kazin, nanna,
    pastizz, mela, sur, kunjata, festa, karozzin, bidwi, ħażen, briju.
  ✔ Maltese phrases in quotes are gold: '"Mela mhux hekk!"', '"Next week, sur."'
  ✔ Deadpan. Understated. Present tense. Short sentences.
  ✔ Adult and cheeky. Innuendo, hangovers, in-law warfare, marital fatigue,
    "in your pants", "who dropped you off" — all fair game.
  ✔ Punch UP or SIDEWAYS: at ministers, contractors, landlords, bureaucracy,
    developers, the tourist board, your own family.
  ✔ Self-deprecating. The Maltese are laughing at themselves. That is the licence.

  ✘ NO sexually explicit content. Suggestion yes, description never.
  ✘ NO slurs, racism, homophobia, misogyny, ableism. Not even "ironically".
  ✘ NO real named people — no politicians, no clubs by name, no businesses,
    no living or dead individuals. 'THE MINISTER' is fine. A name is not.
  ✘ NO punching down: migrants, poverty, disability, religion-as-belief.
    (Religion-as-PROCEDURE — the three-hour procession, the collection plate,
    the altar boy on Red Bull — is fair game and very funny.)
  ✘ NO real brand names. 'Cisk' is already in the shipped set and stays; do not add more.
  ✘ NO politics with a side. Everyone is useless, equally.
  ✘ NO shock for its own sake. Funny beats crude. If it's only crude, cut it.

CALIBRATION — where the line is
  ✔ 'Two hours later you are still on the phone in your pants.'     (cheeky, fine)
  ✔ 'Knows what time you got in. And who dropped you off.'          (innuendo, fine)
  ✔ 'Wants half of everything, including the dog.'                  (adult, fine)
  ✘ anything describing an act                                       (cut it)
  ✘ anything naming a real person or club                            (cut it)
  ✘ anything where the punchline is someone's race or disability     (cut it)

6a. EMOJI SELECTION RULE  (the art field `e`)

  Pick in this order — stop at the first one that works:
    1. THE OBJECT itself         🧨 petard · 🪓 axe · 🩴 slipper · 🧾 bill
    2. THE ANIMAL or FOOD        🐇 rabbit · 🧀 ġbejna · 🐟 lampuki · 🥐 pastizz
    3. THE PERSON doing it       👵 nanna · 👷 contractor · 🎺 bandsman
    4. THE SCENE in one glyph    🚗 traffic · 🏗️ crane · ⛵ luzzu
    5. THE FEELING               🤢 hangover · 💔 the ex · 😈 trouble

  HARD RULES
    ✔ Exactly ONE emoji. Never two. Never an emoji plus text.
    ✔ Must be COLOUR on Android and iOS. Test it — some glyphs render as flat
      black outlines (✔ ✂ ☕-style legacy symbols are risky) and look broken on
      the card. If in doubt pick a newer, obviously-colourful glyph.
    ✔ Prefer wide, high-contrast, instantly readable shapes. The emoji renders at
      ~34 px on a hand card and ~57 px on the full view — detail is lost.
    ✔ Must read at a glance against the card's attribute-tinted vignette:
        festa = warm red · farm = green · city = purple · sea = blue · trouble = slate
      Avoid a red emoji on a festa card or a green one on a farm card — it vanishes.
    ✔ Two cards may share an emoji only if they are in different attributes.
    ✘ Avoid ZWJ sequences (👩‍🌾, 👨‍👩‍👧) where you can — older Androids split them
      into two glyphs. If you must use one, check it renders as a single image.
    ✘ Avoid flags, national symbols, and anything religiously pointed.
    ✘ Avoid skin-tone modifiers entirely.

  QUICK SANITY CHECK: squint at it. If you cannot tell what it is in half a
  second, it is the wrong emoji.
